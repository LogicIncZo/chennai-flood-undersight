The flood machine, opened.

Chennai's ₹107.2-crore flood-forecasting system (RTFF & SDSS) publishes 7 PDF bulletins as its entire public record. Its own GeoServer quietly serves 344 data layers — 1.7 million sensor readings, flood footprints from 2005 and 2015, vendor contracts — to anyone who asks. We mirrored it, published it, and now we audit it.
